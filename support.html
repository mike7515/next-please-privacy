Next, Please! Support

Help for the Next, Please! game for iPhone

Contact

For questions, problems or ideas write to mike7515.dev@gmail.com. Please tell us your iPhone model and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.

My progress on a new iPhone

Your progress is copied to your iCloud. On a new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple Account, keep iCloud on for the game and open it: your coins, stars and upgrades come back in a few seconds. "Remove ads" comes back with Settings (the gear) → "Restore purchases".

Start again from zero

Settings (the gear) → "Reset progress". This also clears the copy in iCloud and cannot be undone. Your language and "Remove ads" are kept.

Ads and privacy

To change what ads may use: Settings → Privacy → Ad choices. To stop tracking: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. More in the Privacy Policy.

Language, sounds and reminders

All in Settings (the gear): language, vibration, sounds, music and reminders.
