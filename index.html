Next Please! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This page explains what data the Next Please! game for iPhone and Android uses, why, and who it is shared with.

Who is responsible

Michele Melluso, independent developer. Contact: nextplease.game@gmail.com

What the game saves

Your progress (coins, levels, stars, upgrades) and settings (language, vibration) stay only on your phone. There is no account and no server of ours, and we do not receive this data. You can delete it with "Reset progress" in Settings or by removing the app.

Ads

The game shows video ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited for people in the European Economic Area and Switzerland, Google LLC elsewhere). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your phone's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android), only if you allow it;
  • your IP address and the approximate location it reveals;
  • device information (model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads (for example whether a video was watched to the end).

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • In Europe and the UK, a Google window asks for your consent the first time you open the game. If you accept, ads can be personalised. If you refuse, you see non-personalised or limited ads and the game works just the same. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad choices.
  • On iPhone, Apple asks whether the app may track you. If you say no, Google does not receive the advertising identifier. You can change this in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Google → Ads.

The legal basis for personalised ads is your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)).

Purchases

Purchases inside the game, such as "Remove ads", go through Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment details: knowing what you bought is used only to unlock it.

Children

The game is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data about them.

Your rights

If you live in the European Union you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Since we keep no data about you, for ad data the Google tools above apply. For any question write to nextplease.game@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top is updated.
